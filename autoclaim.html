What it does

What gets claimedYour delegation (WFLR / FTSO) and staking (P-chain mirror) rewards — the reward types Flare's autoClaim supports.
Where rewards goYour own wallet (or your Flare delegation account, if you enabled one), wrapped as WFLR. The payout address comes from Flare's ClaimSetupManager contract — the executor has no way to redirect it.
Fee per claim0.1 FLR, deducted from the claimed rewards. This is minFeeValueWei — the lowest fee the ClaimSetupManager contract allows any executor to charge. One claim covers all your accumulated unclaimed epochs at once, so the fee is at most 0.1 FLR per epoch and less if rewards accumulate across epochs.
One-time enrollment cost0.1 FLR, attached automatically by the portal when you add the executor. Flare's contract pays it to a newly added registered executor from that transaction — it is set by the protocol, not by us.
Fee changesCapped by the protocol (0.1–100 FLR range), and any change an executor makes is delayed 3 reward epochs (~10.5 days) by the contract — no surprise pricing.
CustodyNone. No token approvals, no deposits, no smart-contract allowances. The executor can only trigger claims that pay you.
LeavingAny time, from the Flare portal, without asking us. Manual claiming always keeps working — whichever happens first simply wins; nothing can be claimed twice.
Fee honesty. 0.1 FLR is the protocol minimum, and we are not the only ones at it: as of 2026-08-15 (read from the chain), Flare has 18 registered executors — CashLab now included — charging between 0.1 and 5 FLR per claim (median 0.15), and nine of the 18 charge the same 0.1 minimum. Other executors can change their fees over time, so this count is a dated snapshot. We match the floor; we don't claim to beat it. Registering as a public executor also burns a one-time 1,000 FLR protocol fee — that is CashLab's cost, never yours.

Small balances — the contract's rule, stated plainly

Flare's contract only permits an executor claim when your accumulated rewards are at least the 0.1 FLR fee. Below that, we cannot auto-claim for you — your rewards simply keep accumulating until they cross the threshold, and you can always claim any amount yourself, fee-free, in the portal. Remember Flare's claim window: rewards left unclaimed expire after about 90 days, whether or not an executor is set.

How to verify it's really us

Before you enroll with any executor — ours included — you should be able to answer: how do I know this address is really theirs? Here is how, and here is why the stakes are smaller than they look.

First, what an executor can never do. Flare's RewardManager contract always sends claimed rewards to your own wallet (or your delegation account, if you enabled one), minus only the executor's fee. The executor has no way to choose a destination — the payout address comes from the ClaimSetupManager contract, not from the executor. Even a malicious executor cannot touch your principal or redirect your rewards. The worst a fake address can do is overcharge you a fee — it can never take your rewards.

So the one thing to check at enrollment is the fee. When your wallet asks you to confirm the enrollment transaction, it shows the amount attached — that amount is the executor's current fee, paid once at enrollment, and the same fee is what each later claim deducts. Ours is exactly 0.1 FLR. The protocol lets an executor charge up to 100 FLR, so a scam executor's game is a high fee — the confirmation shows it before you sign. If it says anything other than 0.1 FLR, stop and don't sign.

Second, don't take the address from one place — including this website. If this site were ever compromised, the page (and the enrollment checker above) could show you a wrong address. That is not paranoia, it is how you should treat every operator's website. Before enrolling, cross-check the executor address in independent places:

Our X account@CashLabNetwork — at launch we will publish the executor address in a pinned announcement post. The address there and the address here must match exactly.
The chain itselfThe executor's registration is an on-chain transaction anyone can read: the registration transaction and the executor's address page on the Flare explorer (chain-verified 2026-08-15). The address in that transaction and the address on this page must match exactly.
The Flare portalThe portal checks the address for you. When you paste an executor address into its Set Executor field, the portal itself reads Flare's ClaimSetupManager contract live and tells you whether the address is a registered executor and what fee it charges — for ours it must say registered, with a 0.1 FLR fee. That confirmation comes from the chain, not from this website, so it works even if you trust nothing we publish. (The portal shows all executors this way — by address, not by name.) If the portal does not confirm the address as a registered executor, or shows any fee other than 0.1 FLR, stop.

And re-read the red caution above this section: however you enroll, setClaimExecutors replaces your whole executor list, so check the full list you are signing, not just one entry.

How to leave

Open the same Set Executor section in the portal and set a new executor list without us (or an empty one). That's it — no notice period, no permission from CashLab needed. Your rewards and your ability to claim them yourself are never affected by enrolling or leaving.
