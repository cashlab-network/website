Why you can check us before trusting us. You never send tokens to CashLab on either path — delegation and staking are on-chain assignments controlled only by your keys, and no legitimate provider will ever ask you to transfer FLR. Beyond that, don't take our word for anything: every settled epoch — including the one that paid zero — is published with numbers derived from Flare's official settlement data; our validator, entity, and delegation address are verifiable on-chain; our failures are logged publicly in the operations log; and this site reads live figures from the chain in your own browser, not from our servers.
