Delegator service

Never click “claim” again.

Enroll once in the Flare portal and our executor delivers your delegation and staking rewards to your own wallet as WFLR every epoch — automatically, on-chain, non-custodial. The payout address comes from Flare's own ClaimSetupManager contract; the executor cannot redirect it. Flat fee per claim: 0.25 FLR — scheduled on-chain in advance, at our measured gas cost, not a margin.

Non-custodial by design

Delegators keep full control of their FLR. Rewards are split by the protocol and distributed automatically — each delegator claims their own share directly via RewardManager Merkle claims. CashLab never holds or routes delegator funds.

You keep your keys
On fees: CashLab charges the protocol minimum of 20%. Under Flare's FIP.16 (April 2026), 20% is the network-wide minimum fee floor — not a ceiling — enforced on-chain. No registered provider can durably charge less; rates below the floor from before enforcement expire with those validators' current terms.
Reliability positioning

Built to stay live. Here's exactly how.

No superlatives, no unverifiable claims — just what we've built and what we've committed to.

  • Dedicated hardware

    A single dedicated Hetzner AX-line server — AMD EPYC 7502P, 128 GB RAM, RAID1 NVMe. No shared cloud tenancy for the validator or the FSP client stack.
