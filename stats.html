Nothing on this page is typed in by hand. The two left tiles are point-in-time values the publish pipeline derives at generation time — active stake read live from Flare's PChainStakeMirror contract, feed count from our machine-readable control plane — and stamps with the moment they were read. The two right tiles, and everything below, are settled per-epoch data from epochs.json. The pipeline regenerates the whole file each epoch, so this page updates itself as epochs settle; each tile's exact source is in its data-source attribute and in epochs.json itself.
